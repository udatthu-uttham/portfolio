# Ride finesse — making the pointer effects premium (plan, 2026-09-17)

**Status: implemented and validated 2026-09-17 (phases A–E; 35/35 headless; reviewed; live §6 checks done). Uttham approved both §5 decisions: quieter ladder, air survives a click or scroll. Validation in §7.** Uttham: "the pointer effects don't look premium".
Method: five craft skills (pointer-trail-emitter, animation-principles, Emil Kowalski design engineering,
gooey-blob-system, ambient-section-particles) applied through three audits of `pointer-ride.js`, three
independent design directions, and a three-judge panel. Builds on `plans/014`; all its locked decisions
stay (native cursor, faint marks allowed on paper text and objects, 8 s hold + 3 s fade, no erase on click,
grain at 0.07, touch and reduced motion get nothing, rubber never on glass or controls).

**Premium, defined for this site:** coherent with the hand-made paper world, near-silent by default,
finished in the invisible details. Not more effect. The rare burnout is the one moment allowed to delight.

## 2. Direction — "Charcoal dust" (unanimous winner, with grafts)

The smoke stops being a blurred blob and becomes **graphite dust the rear tyre lifts off the sheet**: sprites
baked from the paper's own noise, emitted per unit of paper crossed, never swelling, hanging in the air with
a damped coast, a slow curl and a little buoyancy before softening into haze. The rubber becomes **residue**:
denser core, granular edge decided by the same noise, aligned to the path, tapering as the tyre lets go.

Two greys, one rule: **`--graphite` for anything the ride leaves in the air, `--ink-900` for anything on
the sheet.** The hero's `--ink-600` exhaust stays the illustration's and is not a pointer colour.

**Hero coherence — unify the physics, not the medium.** The hero scene is untouched (`plans/014` §10 and your
scope rule). What the pointer deliberately shares with the bikes' exhaust: the alpha ceiling (0.16, as the
new `--dust-alpha`), the drift family (back and up, ≈ 1.2 : 1), the leaving curve, and the restraint of one
puff per stretch of road. A headless identity check reads the hero's literals from `SignalLoop.astro` and
asserts `DUST` matches — a test, never an edit.

**Stopping.** The hand stops → emission stops the same instant. Motes already in the air coast to rest
within ~400 ms, rise a few px, fray on the curl, crossfade to haze and are gone in ≤ 1.3 s. No idle breath,
no last-wisp timer: still means nothing, as locked.

**Tokens.** New: `--dust-alpha: 0.16` (the ceiling for anything airborne over the paper; equals the hero's
literal). Reused: `--graphite`, `--ink-900`, `--dur-1`, `--linger`, `--z-surface-fx`. The paper's noise
frequency stays a module constant with a comment in `global.css` naming it (a `:root` value only JS reads
would be a documented pointer, not a single source).

**Cost.** Bake: two dust sprites and four tread sprites decoded once at init (`img.onload` → `drawImage`,
`requestIdleCallback` with a `setTimeout` fallback), re-baked only when the backing ratio changes.
Frame while dust is airborne: ≤ 64 motes × ≤ 2 `drawImage` of ≤ 28² px; transforms skipped when a mote is
round. Idle: zero, as today.

## 3. Rejected, with reasons

- **The goo cloud** (blur → alpha-threshold on a third canvas). A recognisably web-native metaball idiom,
  grain-free at the most visible moment in a world whose whole argument is tooth and fibres; Safari
  rasterises `url()` filters on the CPU every paint; the runtime fallback pops mid-burst. Unanimous reject.
- **Any edit to the hero scene** (life 650 → 600, gradient fill, importing a shared puff module). Locked
  by `plans/014` §10 and by your scope rule; a "visual no-op" is still an edit to approved art.
- **Switching pointer smoke to `--ink-600`.** Graphite is the site's pencil; the visitor's residue belongs
  to the pencil. At ≤ 0.16 alpha the two greys are barely distinguishable anyway.
- **Idle breath / last-wisp timer, custom cursor, per-frame `ctx.filter` or `shadowBlur`, size growth,
  a 1.4 s burst window, halving dab spacing in a burst, a JS-only `:root` frequency token.**

## 7. Implemented, reviewed and validated (2026-09-17)

**Files.** `src/scripts/pointer-ride.js` rewritten (quiet `RIDE`, frozen `DUST`, distance emission via `owed()`, rear-tyre lift point, closed-form drag/buoyancy/curl, grain→haze crossfade with no swell, staggered deterministic bloom with an alive cap, burst clock in the sampler, release taper, `stopRiding` vs `clearSmoke`, SVG-noise dust bake with canvas fallback, tread baked at drawn size × ratio, one seeded LCG); `src/styles/global.css` (`--dust-alpha: 0.16`, comment naming `PAPER_TOOTH`); `docs/design-tokens.md` / `.json`; `tmp/verify-pointer-ride.mjs` (32 checks).

**Departures from §1–2, all small.** The burnout detector keeps its own rung (`BURST_SPEED 1.15`) rather than reading the raised `RIDE.fast` — otherwise the quieter ladder would also make the burnout hard to earn; documented as a distinct constant. `--graphite`'s 0.85 alpha is not multiplied into the dust ceiling: the airborne peak is `--dust-alpha` 0.16 alone so it stays identical to the hero exhaust, and the speck colour is graphite's rgb. The bloom is gated on controls like every other emission. Round motes are drawn with no transform at all (no rotation drift; variety comes from three grain variants, the grain→haze crossfade and the per-mote curl phase). The clock motes after the bloom are seeded ± 12 px (`DUST.burstClockSpread`), tighter than the bloom's ± 24, because the bike has moved on from the seat. The tread's edge is decided by seeded value noise on a lattice one tooth wide (1 / `PAPER_TOOTH`), the sheet's frequency rather than its exact field. The dust threshold landed at the noise median (`16R − 7.5`) inside a disc solid to 70 % of its radius: mean alpha 0.36 inside the disc, 27 % fully opaque specks, speck colour exactly `--graphite` — about 0.057 mean coverage per mote at peak, against the direction's 0.07 target.

**Review (three lenses, adversarially verified, 2026-09-17).** Eleven findings confirmed and fixed, four disputed and resolved: `settle()` now ends the burnout and forgets the last reversal (a pause inside the window no longer resumes with forced slip, a dumped backlog and 0.18 rubber at a crawl; an old reversal can no longer seat a bloom far from the pointer); the burst clock owes motes only across a continuous sample and spaces them over their tick; a scroll stops the bike once per gesture, not once per Lenis frame; the haze is the first grain tile blurred, not a different seed; three grain variants are baked; the crossfade weights sum to one so the hold is flat; the raster of every SVG bake is checked for colour and coverage so a blank or unfiltered disc falls back to canvas grain instead of shipping; a devicePixelRatio change without a resize rebakes; the gate being closed (touch, reduced motion) skips sizing and baking altogether; the ratio is quantised so a window drag above the area cap does not rebake per event.

**Headless (35/35):** slow and ordinary (1.2 px/ms) moves lay nothing; a 2.4 px/ms flick lifts dust every 72 px on the path from behind the tyre with no rubber and no burnout; the same 600 px path at 2.4 and 4.8 px/ms yields the same count (6 and 6); a gentle arc lays nothing; a sharp corner lays a 190 px scuff at 0.036–0.10 with no floor, then exactly two release dabs at ×0.5 and ×0.25; a reversal skids; a zigzag triggers once with a 14-mote bloom seated at the last reversal, then 34 clock motes over 350 ms, rubber at 0.18, cooldown holding; glass gets dust and no rubber; controls get nothing; gaps and teleports settle; dab spacing 6; coverage saturates, refuses and releases; two identical traces give identical rides; a pause inside the burnout ends it; an old reversal cannot seat one after a re-entry; a 150 ms hesitation owes no backlog.

**Live (pane at 1024×768, real event path, fresh load):** no console or server errors; both canvases mounted at ratio 1.5; all three grain variants and the haze rasterise with a colour error under one level against `--graphite`; the tread with tooth-scale value noise keeps 46–58 % of the capsule solid. A 3 px/ms flick lifts faint motes peaking at 0.11 coverage. A zigzag fires the burnout: darkest pixel under the cloud 0.50 (target ≤ 0.5, tuned from 0.57 by widening the bloom to ± 24 px, the clock to ± 12 and capping alive motes at 32), rubber under it 0.35 (target ≈ 0.4, tuned from 0.53 by lowering the cell cap to 0.2 once the solid tread stacked higher than the old bitten one). The 2× overlay shows grainy graphite discs over a thin broken skid, and a short dashed scuff at a corner. Dust lifted by a flick was still on screen after a wheel scroll had moved the page 240 px, and gone 1.5 s later. The scroll handler stops the bike once per gesture with a 400 ms window, so a smoothing tail with sparse scroll events does not stop it repeatedly.

**Test harness notes.** Synthetic traces need the pane's animation frames running and the `document.hidden` flag overridden for the test; a programmatic `scrollTo` while Lenis animates produces a long fight of sparse scroll events, and a hot-replaced module disposes the ride without remounting — every valid live run was on a fresh load.

**Re-tune after Uttham's first look (2026-09-17, later).** "Most of the time I am not noticing the effect." The quieter ladder had overshot: `fast 1.8 px/ms` is above ordinary mouse speed, so exhaust almost never fired, and `--dust-alpha 0.16` with a 0.36-mean sprite gave ~0.06 coverage per mote. Now: `cruise 0.6 · fast 0.9 · grip 1.6 · sideways 0.5 · minSlip 0.2`; motes every **10 px** (was 72), 14–22 px, born half haze, `--dust-alpha 0.3` — at 2× the trail is a continuous soft grainy ribbon peaking at 0.34 coverage on a 1.25 px/ms move. Rubber: `0.06 + 0.14·slip`, width `3 + 4·slip`, burst 0.22, cell cap back to 0.26 (stacks to ≈ 0.5). The restraint argument (§1 row 10) is superseded by the owner's judgement: he wants a small exhaust on every purposeful move and marks on abrupt turns. A dev-only `window.__pointerRide.peek()` (stripped from production) exposes the live state for the harness.

**Settled (2026-09-18).** After a day of use Uttham found the re-tune "overwhelming" and asked for the judges' restraint back, slightly: `fast 1.2`, `sideways 0.55`, `minSlip 0.25`, spacing 16 px, motes 13–19 px, `--dust-alpha 0.22`, rubber `0.04 + 0.12·slip` (burst 0.2). Unhurried moves (≈1 px/ms) lay nothing; a brisk move trails a soft open ribbon.
