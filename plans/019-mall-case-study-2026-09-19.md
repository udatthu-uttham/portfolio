## 2. What the site has today

One tile and one case page, `meesho-mall-gold`: kicker *Vision*, years *2022—*, title *Meesho Mall & Gold*,
one-line summary, one focus sentence, three scope chips, a glass artboard that says "artwork being

focus + scope · note. Mall and Gold are bundled.

## 3. Proposal

**Split Mall out as its own study** ("Meesho Mall", kicker *Vision*, 2021—). Gold becomes a separate tile
later or folds into a line in Mall's scope; the deck has nothing on Gold.

**Give the case template optional chapters**, so a study can carry a story below the hero without changing
the other three pages: each chapter is an eyebrow (the ruler voice), a heading, one short paragraph, and an
optional figure on a glass artboard. Studies without chapters render exactly as today.

**Mall's chapters, five beats from thirty slides** (headings are placeholders in the site's voice; Uttham
approves every word):
1. **The bet** — what Mall is, Oct 2021, the ask: 0.9 % → 5 % of NMV. *Figure: v1 tags on cards.*
2. **Bold wasn't enough** — v2's comms and journey points, the 2.9 % and the award, then flat; seller labels
   diluting the promise. *Figure: v2 PLP pill and PDP.*
3. **The turn** — research: the name meant company products, USPs didn't matter, tick + purple = trust. The
   "no amount of blue" line as the pull-quote on paper (amber highlighter on the key phrase).
4. **Colour as trust** — v3: entry, education, splash, FTUX, PDP enclosure, landing, confirmation. *Figure: a
   strip of three v3 screens.*
5. **What it did** — the three numbers as plain type on paper (not stat tiles), the Lucknow verbatim as a
   sticky note, and one line on what's next. The People slide maps to the site's "How I lead" sheet — a
   one-line cross-reference, not a repeat.

**Materials rule.** Text on paper; phone screens on glass artboards; quotes as handwriting or highlighter;
numbers in the interface sans. No purple: the app's colour lives inside the screenshots only.
